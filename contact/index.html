Emergency Breakdown Safety Tips

✓ DO'S
  • • Switch on hazard warning lights immediately.
  • • Move your car safely to the side of the road if possible.
  • • Share your exact WhatsApp live location with us.
  • • Keep your vehicle ignition off while waiting.
✕ DON'TS
  • • Don't keep cranking a dead starter motor repeatedly.
  • • Don't pour cold water over an overheated engine block.
  • • Don't leave headlights on full beam while engine is off.
  • • Don't stand directly behind your car on busy roads.
